IoT stands for Internet of Things — a network of physical objects ("things") that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies so they can collect, send, and receive data over the internet.

These “things” can be anything:

Everyday devices — smartwatches, thermostats, light bulbs, refrigerators

Industrial machines — manufacturing robots, energy meters, connected vehicles

Environmental sensors — weather stations, pollution monitors, agricultural moisture sensors

Key idea: IoT connects the physical world to the digital world so devices can work autonomously and exchange data without requiring direct human input every time.
