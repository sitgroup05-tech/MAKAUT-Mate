# MAKAUT-Mate
MAKAUT-Mate is your AI-powered academic companion for MAKAUT. This multi-agent system simplifies student life by managing schedules, tracking your syllabus, finding resources, and sending timely alerts. It's designed to help you stay organized, informed, and ahead in your studies. 🤖
